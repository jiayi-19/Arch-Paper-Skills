# Empirical style advice

Use `style_metrics.py FILE --section abstract|introduction` to compare lexical features
with complete development sections. Words, sentence boundaries, phrase dictionaries,
quantiles and proxy definitions are implemented in `writing_metrics.py`. Draft sentence
splitting is heuristic; corpus boundaries were source-inspected. Passive-voice and
trailing-participial counts are lexical proxies, not grammatical ground truth.

An outlier is a revision question, not an error. Keep necessary technical detail,
scope qualifications and author voice even if they differ from this small selected
sample. No hard ban on punctuation, limitations, hypotheses or short sentences is
inferred from award status. Do not optimize text merely to imitate a percentile.

Prefer actors, operations and relationships to vague benefit words. State what a
controller compares, what a buffer retains, why a schedule changes and what outcome
was measured. Merge needless repetition while preserving cause and evidence. Place
important conditions with their claim; detailed setup can remain in methodology.
