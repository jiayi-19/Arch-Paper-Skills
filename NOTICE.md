# Attribution and adaptation

This skill adapts the workflow ideas of [Best-Paper-Writing-Skill](https://github.com/gulucaptain/Best-Paper-Writing-Skill), inspected at Git revision `b9eff99`: routing by contribution, an evidence-backed claim map, source-located exemplar retrieval, figure planning and draft checks. Its original material is CC BY 4.0.

The upstream project credits [Master-cai/Research-Paper-Writing-Skills](https://github.com/Master-cai/Research-Paper-Writing-Skills) (MIT, copyright 2026 Master-cai) and [Peng Sida's research notes](https://github.com/pengsida/learning_research) for parts of its workflow. Their attribution is retained; the base MIT license is included as `LICENSE-base-skill`.

Changes: a new architecture-specific corpus from HPCA/ASPLOS/ISCA/MICRO; new analytical cards; architecture measurement and attribution guidance; a shorter scope-adaptive workflow; new standard-library retrieval and source-checking tools. CV/ML corpus statistics and template assets were not imported. Author preferences that suppress all limitations in abstracts, enforce punctuation or sentence quotas, or require local rendering were not retained.

Original architecture-specific text and scripts in this adaptation are licensed CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Preserve credit and indicate modifications when redistributing. No endorsement by the upstream authors, paper authors, award committees or conferences is implied.

Short attributed paper excerpts remain the property of their respective rights holders. They are included for commentary and instruction and are not covered by the adaptation's license. Learn their argument function; do not copy them into new papers. Award metadata and paper analysis have different sources: awards from official listings, passages from papers, analysis from this skill's curator.
